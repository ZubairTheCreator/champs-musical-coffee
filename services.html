What wedo.

Four services. The shop you can walk into, the bar we bring to you, the coffee you take home, and the training that puts you behind the machine.

The coffeeshop.

Espresso, flat whites, cappuccinos, mochas. Poured to order at 5 Cabral Street, Forest Glade, and finished by hand, which is why most of the photographs on this site are of the top of a cup.

Sit in with it or take it with you. Phone ahead if you are driving out to us.

Call 061 495 6539 Get directions

Coffeecatering.

We set up and pour at your venue. Weddings, church mornings, corporate events, birthdays, launches. Your guests get the same cup they would get in the shop.

We cover Eerste River, Kuils River, Stellenbosch, Paarl, Franschhoek and greater Cape Town. Tell us the date, the venue and roughly how many people, and we will tell you what it takes.

Ask for a catering quote

Coffeeproducts.

We resell coffee products, so the cup you liked here is one you can make at home. Stock moves, so ask us at the counter or over the phone for what is in at the moment.

Ask what is in stock See the coffee

Baristatraining.

We train people to work a machine properly. Grinding and dosing, extraction, steaming milk, latte art, and keeping the equipment clean enough to keep the coffee tasting right. Hands on, on our bar, with the owner.

Finish the training and you get your certificate as a barista. Whether you want it for a job, for your own shop, or just to make a better cup at home, phone the owner and we will work out the dates and what it costs.
