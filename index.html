Four things,done well.

We have been pouring in Eerste River since 2015. The shop is the heart of it; the catering bar and the retail side grew out of people asking whether they could have it somewhere else, or at home.

The coffee shop

Espresso, flat whites, cappuccinos and mochas, poured to order at 5 Cabral Street, Forest Glade. Sit in, or take it with you.

Coffee catering

We bring the bar to you. Weddings, church mornings, corporate events and functions, across the northern suburbs and the winelands.

Coffee products

We resell coffee products, so the cup you liked here is one you can make at home. Ask us at the counter or over the phone.
